Welcome to Sky Son Treasury

The First Subsidiary Venture of Sky Son Labs

Sky Son Labs is the parent brand behind a growing vision for innovation and long-term progress. Sky Son Treasury is its first venture — focused on building a founder-led, crypto-oriented, multi-asset treasury through disciplined research, strategic accumulation and long-term thinking.

Investment philosophy

Research deeply. Hold deliberately. Review continuously.

Inspired by the clarity of foundational digital-currency research and the long-term mindset of strategic treasury management.

₿

Bitcoin-first reserve thinking

Study Bitcoin's monetary design, scarcity model, network security and long-term role without assuming guaranteed appreciation.

⌛

Long-term holding discipline

Prefer a multi-year investment horizon, while retaining the ability to reassess assets when fundamentals change.

◈

Selective digital-asset exposure

Evaluate projects by utility, security, adoption, token economics, governance and liquidity—not hype alone.

⚖

Diversification & risk control

Recognize volatility, concentration risk, custody risks, regulatory uncertainty and the possibility of permanent loss.

◎

Secure custody practices

Use documented wallet controls, independent backups, access separation and periodic security reviews.

↻

Periodic portfolio review

Review long-term holdings and thesis changes on a scheduled basis rather than holding every asset blindly.
